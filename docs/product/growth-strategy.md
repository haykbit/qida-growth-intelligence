# Six-month growth strategy

All impact figures in the application are editable planning assumptions over the stated baseline of 2,000 monthly SQL, not real Qida outcomes. They must be recalibrated with observed experiments.

## Prioritized bets

1. **Activate high-demand, serviceable municipalities through the existing landing factory.** Use opportunity evidence and capacity gates to select local pages. Marketing owns content, Operations confirms coverage, MarTech connects publishing/measurement, Data validates cohorts, and Head of AI governs generation. Validate incremental qualified SQL with municipal treatment/control groups.
2. **Repair end-to-end identity and CRM traceability.** Deterministic source mappings, opportunity-level funnel counting, unattributed-event monitoring, and platform/CRM reconciliation enable every later investment decision. MarTech and Data lead; Marketing and Operations adopt identifiers/status rules.
3. **Improve SQL → alta in high-volume/low-conversion areas.** Use discard reasons, call themes, response time, and capacity evidence to test operational and message interventions without confusing correlated channel mix with causal effect.
4. **Reallocate inefficient paid spend with CRM outcomes.** Compare platform conversions with linked SQL/altas, preserve unattributed spend, and test reallocations rather than crediting all campaign cost to inferred municipalities.

Deprioritize autonomous CMS publication, probabilistic identity merging, opaque predictive scoring, and national-scale page generation until traceability, capacity, local evidence, and human governance meet their gates.

## First 90 days

- Days 1–30: establish metric catalog, identifiers, audit coverage, baseline cohorts, opportunity rules, and landing validation; select treatment/control municipalities.
- Days 31–60: publish the first human-approved landing experiment, reconcile Ads/CRM, monitor indexation and capacity, and test funnel instrumentation.
- Days 61–90: read mature cohorts, inspect pre-trends and channel cannibalization, continue/modify/stop bets, and prepare the second municipality/service wave.

## Six-month roadmap

- Month 1: trustworthy baseline and ownership.
- Month 2: opportunity ranking and governed landing activation.
- Month 3: first experiment readout and traceability repair.
- Month 4: conversion intervention and paid-media reconciliation.
- Month 5: scale only passing plays; investigate negative/inconclusive results.
- Month 6: portfolio review, rules calibration, production integration decision.

## Decision policy

Continue when traceability, sample maturity, capacity, guardrails, and the primary metric all pass predefined gates. Modify when direction is promising but implementation, pre-trend, attribution, or capacity evidence is weak. Stop when harm/guardrails fail, operational eligibility changes, repeated mature results show no improvement, or measurement cannot distinguish the effect.
