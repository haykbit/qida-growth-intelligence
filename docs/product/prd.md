# Product requirements

## Problem

Growth, operations, and leadership lack a single explainable view connecting local demand, capacity, acquisition, CRM outcomes, customer journeys, landing performance, experiments, and data quality.

## Users and outcomes

- Growth analysts prioritize municipality/service opportunities with evidence and constraints.
- Channel managers reconcile platform performance with CRM SQL and altas.
- Local-landing operators draft, review, approve, publish, and measure safe pages.
- Leadership sees funnel health, traceability, capacity risk, and outcomes.
- Data teams locate incomplete, late, duplicated, and unattributed records.

## MVP surfaces

Executive Overview, Growth Opportunities and detail, Customer Journey Intelligence, Funnel Analytics, Landing Factory, SEO & Landing Performance, Paid Media, Experimentation Lab, Data Quality & Observability, Growth Strategy & Roadmap, Analytics Workbench, Architecture & Governance, and Case Study / Interview Mode. Global filters must change API results.

The product must answer three interview-level questions explicitly: where value exists, how it will be measured, and how it will be built. Strategy planning begins at the stated 2,000 monthly SQL baseline, exposes editable conservative/base/optimistic assumptions, names owners and dependencies, and distinguishes existing, proposed, and locally simulated capabilities.

## Success criteria

The complete seed-to-dashboard vertical slice runs locally; the original and extension data model is present; scenarios A–L are documented/queryable; identity, score, cohort, experiment and publication rules are tested; the UI clearly labels measurement limitations.

It must also demonstrate the first investment and rationale, incremental impact range, required teams, 90-day execution, six-month roadmap, measurement/stop criteria, traceability approach, safe use of the existing landing factory, and response to negative or inconclusive evidence.

## Non-goals

No external LLM, real CMS publishing, probabilistic identity merge, enterprise authentication, real customer data, or automatic causal claims.
